## 2. What the leaders do that we don't

1. **They have crawlable pages per intent; KeyFoby has one URL** (40 visible words in raw HTML, about 555 including hidden Suspense blocks that inline scripts reveal; FAQ answers and reviews absent, `crawl.md` §3). Nation has 6 service pages (759–942 words), Auto Key Pro has 16 service URLs and 8 town pages, Alpha has 8 location pages, and Mr. Key Pro has 3 genuinely local town pages. Auto Key Pro (463 URLs) is organic #1 on 2 of the 3 tracked queries, and Alpha and About Town also make the top 10. KeyFoby is #13 organic for "car locksmith hamilton" and missing from its `pws=0` 3-pack. Evidence: matrix; `gbp-maps.md` §2c.
2. **They put reviews in the HTML.** Auto Key Pro server-renders 5 Google reviews with a count (Trustindex), Mr. Key Pro 8 named cards linked to the GBP, and About Town 10 first-party testimonials with vehicle detail. KeyFoby's 222 reviews are invisible until the user scrolls (Elfsight, IntersectionObserver). Evidence: `competitors/*.md` §6; `crawl.md` §3.
3. **They answer the price question.** CanDo publishes "starting at" prices for 7 services. Auto Key Pro puts a make/model/year price lookup first on mobile, on every page. KeyFoby's prices exist only in JSON-LD and click-only FAQ answers. PAA for "car key replacement hamilton" leads with "How much does it cost to replace a key for my car?". Evidence: `cando-lock-and-key.md` §5; `auto-key-pro.md` §5; `gbp-maps.md` §2c.
4. **They work the GBP.** CanDo posted 6 stories in 5 weeks (technician, vehicle, neighbourhood) and Alpha posts job logs naming towns. Service lists run 10–92 entries vs KeyFoby's 5, Nation has 4 products, and photo counts are 42–104 (Hamilton Prime 173) vs KeyFoby's 29. KeyFoby has 0 posts and 0 products, and no owner reply on any of the last 13 reviews. Evidence: `gbp-maps.md` §1, §3, §5.
5. **They use the "Emergency locksmith service" category.** It is Nation's primary and a secondary for About Town, AKP Van and Alpha. KeyFoby has Locksmith + Key duplication service only. Evidence: Maps place-data category arrays (`gbp-maps.md` §3).
6. **They name a person and back it with verifiable trust.** Alpha names owner Bryce Ferrie, his credentials, his insurer (Desjardins) and a 3-month written warranty in every footer. About Town says "since 1994", names owner Richard and links TAOL/ALOA/CFIB. CanDo names technician Ryan. KeyFoby's site names nobody, although 14 reviews name the technician (Mohammad), and it says "Licensed & Insured" (×3 on the homepage) with no licence number or insurer. Evidence: `alpha-locksmith.md`, `about-town-lock-and-key.md` §6–7; `gbp-maps.md` §1; grep of `app/`, `lib/`, `crawl/*.html` found no technician name.
7. **They keep the call button in reach on mobile.** Auto Key Pro has a sticky bottom "CALL US" bar and Alpha a mobile-only Call Now Button. KeyFoby has only a fixed top nav, and no phone text in the 375 px fold. Evidence: `auto-key-pro.md` §5; `alpha-locksmith.md` CTAs; `crawl.md` §9.
8. **Some buy the top slot.** Hamilton Prime and Diligent Locksmith run Local Services Ads above organic on "car key replacement hamilton" (1 of 2 loads) and "car locksmith hamilton" (both loads). None of the brief's six does. Evidence: `gbp-maps.md` §2c.

## 3. Gaps KeyFoby can own

1. **Make pages with real content.** None of the six has one. Mr. Key Pro's 10 make URLs are empty, Auto Key Pro has only category archives and blog posts, and About Town shows logos. Choose launch makes from search demand and job records, **not** from the "kia key fob (22)" chip: only 1 of those 22 reviews mentions Kia. Reviews name Honda 6, Jeep 3, Nissan 2, Toyota 2. Evidence: matrix; `gbp-maps.md` §1.
2. **Crawlable price ranges by service and make.** CanDo is the only one that shows prices, and they are JS-only on a robots-blocked host. That leaves 0 crawlable price pages in the set for "car key replacement hamilton cost", "cheapest car key replacement hamilton" and the cost PAAs. Reviewers' Google price tags (17; mostly CA$100–150 and CA$200–250) give a sanity check for the Truth Table #5 ranges.
3. **The definitive car-key-replacement page.** The organic #1 (Auto Key Pro) is ~80% lockout copy. Nation's car page has no fob, make or price content (0 hits for "key fob"). About Town's is 238 words, and Alpha's 475 words contradict themselves ("cut and program most auto keys" vs "non-chip key copying"). A page that covers key types, process, ID/VIN, price range and turnaround has no real rival.
4. **One consistent entity.** Every competitor has a NAP, hours or rating conflict: CanDo address and hours, AKP's 3 listings, 2 phones and weekday GBP hours vs a "7 days" site, Alpha's 3 versions of its hours, Nation's 174 vs 173, Mr. Key Pro's 520 vs 633. None of the six ships schema that fully matches its own GBP: three have no LocalBusiness schema, and the other three disagree with it on hours or rating. Once KeyFoby fixes its own conflicts (5 versions of its hours, 3 ratings), clean entity data is a differentiator.
5. **Evenings and weekends, if true (Truth Table #1).** AKP's three listings are weekday-only, About Town is Mon–Fri 10–4, and Alpha's schema and contact page disagree. Reviews already document 5 after-hours and 4 weekend jobs. This is not unique: CanDo, Nation, Mr. Key Pro and Hamilton Prime also show 24 h.
6. **Mobile fast path.** None of the six has SMS. Only CanDo has WhatsApp. Mr. Key Pro has no `tel:` at all. None of the observed forms has a field for a photo of the key. KeyFoby already has WhatsApp, Messenger and a year/make/model selector (`app/components/CarSelector.tsx`, inside the quote form). A sticky call + WhatsApp bar, a crawlable vehicle picker and a photo upload would lead the set.
