## 7. Weak or thin (do not copy)

- No key fob, smart key, make or price content anywhere. This is the clearest opening for KeyFoby.
- 26–48% of service-page copy is shared across pages. 5 sentences repeat verbatim on 6 pages (e.g. "We believe in providing service with a smile.").
- Recycled testimonials attributed to different names. This is the kind of invented proof the brief bans.
- The service-area page names 15 towns, including London, Kitchener and Guelph, with interchangeable two-sentence blurbs and a single 30-minute promise. That is doorway-style coverage on one URL.
- Schema only on the home page, with a hard-coded `aggregateRating` (174 vs GBP 173), a non-standard `openingHours` string and `addressCountry: "Canada"`. No Service, FAQPage or BreadcrumbList.
- Titles lack the city on service pages. Every meta description truncates.
- Sitemap last updated 2024-05-29 and misses `/free-estimate/`. Last GBP post 2026-07-12.
- The decorative drag-slider hero wastes the top of the screen; there is no WhatsApp or SMS; the form uses US "zip code".
