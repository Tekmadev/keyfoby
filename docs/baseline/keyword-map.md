## 2. Headline findings

1. **The brief's 5 related searches check out, with one wording fix.** "cheapest car key replacement hamilton", "mobile car key replacement hamilton", "car locksmith hamilton" and "key cutting hamilton mountain" are echoed. For "car key replacement hamilton cost", autocomplete prefers **"…hamilton prices"**: it is a completion of 3 stems, while "cost" is not echoed.
2. **Hamilton appears inside national completions** for "car key replacement" (#6, beside Ottawa, Brampton, Scarborough, Toronto, London, Mississauga), "key fob replacement" (#3) and "key fob programming" (#4). These are the strongest local signals in the set.
3. **Real Hamilton demand covers only about 6 service ideas.** These are car key replacement or programming, key fob replacement or programming, car key cutting or copy, key fob or car key repair and battery, lost car keys, and locked keys or locked out. Tail-only for Hamilton: transponder, smart, proximity, push-to-start, shell replacement, laser-cut, broken-key extraction, ignition repair, remote-start fob, all-keys-lost, and 24-hour car locksmith.
4. **Every make + Hamilton query is tail-only (28 of 28).** Make demand is national: across 5 non-make question stems, Toyota and Nissan have 8 hits each, Honda and Hyundai 7, Kia 2 (`make_signals.txt`). First-party data does not single out Kia either. The GBP "kia key fob" chip (22 reviews) is Google's label for the fob cluster: only 1 of those 22 mentions Kia. Across all 222 reviews, Honda/Civic is named 6 times, Jeep 3, Nissan 2, Toyota 2, Kia 1, Hyundai 0 (`gbp-maps.md` §1). **No make is presumed first**; the order needs job logs (§4).
5. **Towns.** Burlington and Oakville are echoed for both "car key replacement" and "car locksmith". Grimsby is echoed, but its results are mixed with UK Grimsby. Ancaster, Dundas, Stoney Creek, Waterdown, Binbrook, Flamborough and Caledonia are tail-only.
6. **Customers say "lost car keys", not "all keys lost".** The national completions for "all keys lost" are locksmith tools (Autel KM100, "key programmer"), which is trade intent. For lockouts, "locked keys in car hamilton" is echoed, while "car lockout hamilton" is rewritten to "car locksmith hamilton".
7. **New alternatives show up in the data: Costco and Canadian Tire.** The completions include "car key replacement hamilton costco", "key fob replacement costco canada" and "key fob replacement canadian tire". Car Keys Express began serving Canadian Costco members in Ontario in May 2025 and targets 85% of Canadian warehouses by end of 2026 (newswire.com release, fetched). There is also a cluster of Canadian dealer-price questions ("dealership car key replacement cost ontario / toronto / mississauga").
8. **Brand demand is invisible.** "keyfoby" autocompletes to generic "key fob" terms, and "keyfoby hamilton" is tail-only. The brand reads almost the same as the generic term, and app.keyfoby.com shares the name.
9. **People Also Ask is about money first.** Of the 12 visible PAA questions on the 3 checked SERPs, 5 ask about cost (replace a key ×2, program a fob ×2, "locksmith cost in Hamilton"), 2 about alternatives to the dealer ("Does Home Depot reprogram key fobs?"), 2 about lockouts, 1 about programming without the original, 1 about Ontario locksmith licensing and 1 about remote starters (`gbp-maps.md` §2c). All 12 are now mapped (CSV `source=PAA`) and reorder §8.
